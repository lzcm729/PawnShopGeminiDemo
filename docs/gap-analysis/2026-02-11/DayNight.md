### Key Gaps

**Missing:**
- **#44 Cross-phase uncertainty sharing**: The design doc explicitly states that day-time appraisal and night-time insight (格物) operate on the same `uncertainty` value, and that insight's reduction should benefit next-day negotiations. Currently, the insight system produces essence but does NOT modify item uncertainty. This is a meaningful gameplay loop gap since it breaks the designed synergy between daytime and nighttime activities.

**Divergent:**
- **#6, #30 Mail accessibility during day**: The design doc says mail is inaccessible during business hours (grayed out or notification only). The current implementation renders MailModal during all phases including BUSINESS/NEGOTIATION/DEPARTURE. This weakens the designed "information buffer" effect where mail reading is reserved for the reflective night phase.

**Partial:**
- **#22 Story-specific bedtime monologues**: Only one chain-specific bedtime thought (police risk). Design envisions reflections tied to specific story events (e.g., "today I refused Emma's item").
- **#24, #27 Transition audio**: Shutter sound plays, but ambient crossfade (day street noise -> night quiet) is commented out ("Removed per user request"). Night-to-day alarm sound is also commented out and undefined.
- **#34 Auto events at night start**: Design specifies three auto-event categories shown before free activities (settlement, business summary, item status). Currently, maintenance deduction happens during sleep transition, and day summary is shown in the monologue screen, not as a distinct pre-activity phase.
