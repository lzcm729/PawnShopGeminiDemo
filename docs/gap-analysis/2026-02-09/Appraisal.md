### Missing/Partial Details

**#22 (Missing) A3: NPC Dialogue Attitude by Style**
The design specifies that NPC negotiation styles (cunning, stubborn, soft, calm) should exhibit different dialogue behaviors at different uncertainty levels. For example, a cunning NPC should "drop the act" at low uncertainty, while a stubborn NPC should "soften attitude but hold bottom line." Currently, NPC dialogue is independent of item uncertainty -- the `getPushPullStyle` function maps to negotiation mechanics (concession chance/amount) but not dialogue content.

**#26 (Missing) Forfeiture Settlement via Black Market Price**
The design document (section 3.4 C') explicitly states: "Forfeiture auto-settlement = sold at that day's black market sale price for the item. Uncertainty's influence is naturally transmitted through market volatility." However, the current implementation in `useGameEngine.ts:1215-1218` uses a fixed formula: `realValue * LIQUIDATION_RATE (0.80) * marketEffects`. This does not incorporate the item's uncertainty-based volatility from the black market system.

**#11 (Partial) Trait Leverage Numeric Tiers**
The design specifies distinct tiers: minor flaw -5% (no patience cost), severe flaw -15% (patience -1), fake = collapse (patience -1). The implementation uses per-trait `valueImpact` from CSV data which is more flexible, but there is no patience cost for using severe FLAW or FAKE leverage. The patience cost aspect of trait leverage is not implemented.

**#27 (Partial) Precision-Based Instinct Text Stages**
The design specifies 5 distinct stages for precision-aware merchant instinct: (1) entering negotiation, (2) NPC concedes, (3) NPC deadlocks, (4) deal closes, (5) black market browsing. Only stage (1) is implemented with generic low/high uncertainty texts, and even that fires probabilistically rather than consistently.
